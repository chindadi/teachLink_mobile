# Contributor Offboarding Process

## Purpose and Scope

This process provides a respectful, secure transition when a contributor
voluntarily leaves, changes roles, or no longer needs project access. Conduct
enforcement and involuntary privilege removal also follow the applicable
[Revocation Policy](../policies/REVOCATION.md) or
[Code of Conduct Enforcement Policy](../COC_ENFORCEMENT.md).

## Steps

1. **Confirm the transition.** The contributor informs the role owner or a
   maintainer, identifies the effective date, and notes whether they are
   resigning, taking leave, or changing roles. A person may ask for a private
   conversation; no explanation is required for voluntary departure.
2. **Plan handover.** The role owner and contributor list active work, pending
   decisions, incidents, deadlines, and named successors. Transfer project-owned
   documentation, credentials, and operational knowledge using approved secure
   channels. Never transfer personal credentials or expose secrets in issues or
   pull requests.
3. **Review access.** A maintainer inventories repository, organization, CI,
   release, signing, cloud, and communication access associated with the role.
   Remove access no longer required by the effective date. Rotate shared
   credentials or revoke tokens when exposure or ownership requires it, using
   the relevant security procedures.
4. **Close responsibilities.** Reassign open reviews, issues, release tasks,
   working-group duties, and service ownership. Record unresolved work and
   follow-up owners.
5. **Acknowledge and record.** Confirm completion privately with the departing
   person. Update public role records and project documentation without
   disclosing private reasons or personal information.

## Timing and Exceptions

For a planned departure, the role owner should coordinate the handover before
the effective date, normally within 14 days of notice. Access removal may be
immediate where required for security, safety, or platform compliance. In an
urgent case, prioritize containment and arrange an independent review as soon as
practicable. The transition must not delay necessary security response.

## Ownership

The relevant role owner coordinates offboarding; maintainers are accountable for
repository and governance access. The maintainers own this process and review it
annually.
