# Proposal Lifecycle

## Purpose

This process defines the stages, owners, and exit criteria for substantive
project proposals. Small routine contributions may use the normal issue and
pull-request workflow. A proposal requiring a formal vote also follows
[`FORMAL_VOTING.md`](FORMAL_VOTING.md).

## Stages

| Stage          | Owner                                               | Exit criteria                                                                                                                                     |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Draft          | Proposer                                            | Problem, goals, non-goals, options, impact, and requested decision are stated; open questions are identified.                                     |
| Initial review | Maintainer or delegate                              | Proposal is in scope, has an owner, and is sufficiently clear to invite discussion; otherwise it is returned with specific gaps.                  |
| Discussion     | Proposer, facilitated by an uninvolved maintainer   | Relevant feedback and objections are recorded; the proposer responds and updates the draft or explains why feedback was not adopted.              |
| Decision       | Authority defined by the charter or relevant policy | The decision-maker selects approval, rejection, deferral, or request for revision under the applicable consensus or voting rule.                  |
| Implementation | Named implementation owner                          | Approved work meets the stated acceptance criteria and required reviews and checks. Approval of a proposal does not itself authorize a merge.     |
| Closure        | Proposal owner                                      | Outcome, links to implementation or follow-up work, and any rationale or dissent are recorded; deferred proposals state how they may be reopened. |

## Ownership and Changes

The proposer owns an accurate draft and responses to feedback. A maintainer
coordinates initial review and ensures the correct decision authority is used.
The decision authority owns the decision and its record. The implementation
owner owns delivery and handover. The maintainers own this process and review it
annually.
