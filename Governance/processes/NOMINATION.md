# Role Nomination Process

## Scope

This process governs appointments to project roles documented in
[`Governance/roles/`](../roles/). A role-specific document may add criteria or
steps but may not remove the conflict, notice, and recording safeguards here.

## Nomination

Any current maintainer or role-holder may nominate a person, including
themselves, by opening a public issue. The nominee must consent before the
nomination proceeds. The issue includes the role, nominee, nominator, evidence
against the role criteria, expected term or duties, and any known conflicts. A
second from an uninvolved current maintainer is required within 7 calendar days;
without a second, the nomination is closed or returned for revision.

## Review and Decision

The nomination remains open for at least 14 calendar days after the second. The
nominee may respond, and community members may provide relevant evidence or
concerns. Maintainership appointments and any role whose definition requires
maintainer approval are decided by the maintainers. Other roles are decided by
the authority named in that role's document. Decision-makers disclose conflicts
and recuse where appropriate.

Consensus is preferred. If consensus is not reached, a simple majority decision
may be used when permitted by the governing role document and voting policies.
An appointment requiring a supermajority follows
[`SUPERMAJORITY.md`](../policies/SUPERMAJORITY.md). The decision and rationale
are recorded on the nomination issue, with sensitive details omitted. The role
starts when the decision is recorded and required access is granted.

## Ownership

The maintainers own this process. Changes follow the governance change process.
