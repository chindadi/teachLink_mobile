# Meeting Cadence

## Principles

Meetings exist to make decisions, coordinate work, or resolve issues that are
not served as well asynchronously. Participation is open to relevant project
participants unless a meeting handles private conduct, personnel, or security
information. Use the agenda template and publish notes appropriate to the
meeting's confidentiality.

## Meeting Types

| Meeting                 | Cadence                               | Participants                                     | Purpose                                                                |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Maintainer coordination | Monthly                               | Maintainers; invited owners                      | Priorities, decisions, role coverage, risks, and escalations           |
| Release readiness       | Before each planned release           | Release manager, maintainers, relevant reviewers | Verify release gates, blockers, ownership, and go/no-go recommendation |
| Working group sync      | Set by charter, normally bi-weekly    | Working group members and invited stakeholders   | Progress, blockers, decisions, and deliverables                        |
| Community office hours  | Optional, scheduled by community lead | Open to contributors and users                   | Questions, onboarding, feedback, and community connection              |

The relevant owner may cancel a meeting when there is no decision or useful
agenda, and should provide an asynchronous update instead. Working group cadence
and duration are defined in its approved charter.

## Attendance and Preparation

Invited participants should accept, decline, or send an update if they cannot
attend. Attendance is expected only when a participant owns an agenda item,
decision, or required expertise; absence does not imply approval. Decision
eligibility is determined by the governing policy, not meeting attendance.
Agenda items and supporting material should be shared at least 2 business days
before a scheduled decision meeting when practical.

## Notes and Follow-Up

The facilitator identifies a note-taker and records decisions, dissent where
material, action owners, and due dates. Public notes are linked from the relevant
issue or project record. Sensitive details remain in restricted records, with a
non-sensitive summary when appropriate. The meeting owner tracks actions to
completion or transfers them to the appropriate issue or proposal.

The maintainers review this cadence annually and may adjust schedules based on
project activity and participant time zones.
