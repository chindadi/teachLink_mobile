# Role Promotion Criteria

## Purpose

Promotion means appointment to a role with broader or different project
responsibilities. It is based on evidence of readiness, not tenure alone. The
criteria below complement the specific requirements in each role document.

## Criteria by Role

| Proposed role      | Objective evidence to assess                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Reviewer           | At least 5 merged contributions and 20 constructive reviews; demonstrates domain knowledge, accurate feedback, and reliable review participation.                                                             |
| Triager            | Sustained issue or pull request participation; accurate use of the label taxonomy; responsive, clear routing and prioritization.                                                                              |
| Committer          | At least 6 months of active contribution, 20 merged pull requests, 30 constructive reviews, consistent CI/review/changelog compliance, and trusted judgment.                                                  |
| Maintainer         | Sustained cross-project stewardship; sound technical and governance decisions; reliable review, release, security, or community work; demonstrated accountability and ability to resolve disagreement fairly. |
| Working Group Lead | Relevant delivery experience; a feasible charter; clear communication, coordination, reporting, and handover evidence.                                                                                        |
| Other defined role | Evidence must map directly to the duties, limits, and appointment criteria in that role's document.                                                                                                           |

Counts are minimum evidence where specified, not an automatic entitlement or a
substitute for quality. Role-specific documents take precedence when they set a
higher or more specific standard. All nominees must accept the role's duties and
limits and be in good standing under applicable project policies.

## Nominators and Approvers

Nominations follow the [Role Nomination Process](NOMINATION.md), including
nominee consent, a second from an uninvolved maintainer, and a 14-day discussion
period. The maintainers approve maintainer appointments and any appointment
assigned to them by a role document. Other appointments are approved by the
authority stated in the relevant role document. Conflicted decision-makers
recuse. Any required supermajority follows the
[Supermajority Policy](../policies/SUPERMAJORITY.md).

## Evidence and Record

The nomination must link to verifiable evidence such as merged work, reviews,
triage history, delivered milestones, or documented role duties. The approver
records how the evidence meets the criteria and the decision on the nomination
issue. Private or security-sensitive material is summarized without disclosing
its contents.

The maintainers own these shared criteria and review them annually.
