# Formal Voting Process

## When a Formal Vote Is Required

A formal vote is used when consensus cannot be reached on a decision that the
charter assigns to the maintainers, when a governing policy requires a vote, or
when the decision authority explicitly submits a question to a vote. Lazy
consensus must not replace a required formal vote.

## Eligibility and Notice

Eligible voters are the non-conflicted maintainers, except where a specific
policy defines another electorate. The vote notice states the exact motion,
decision context, eligible group, applicable threshold, opening and closing time,
and where votes are recorded. Provide at least 7 calendar days for voting.
Extensions and changes to the motion must be announced; a materially changed
motion restarts the notice period.

Quorum is determined by the [Quorum Policy](../policies/QUORUM.md). Simple
majority, tie resolution, and any required supermajority follow the respective
[Supermajority](../policies/SUPERMAJORITY.md) and
[Tie-Breaking](../policies/TIE_BREAKING.md) policies. A non-quorate vote does
not decide the motion.

## Voting and Recording

Each eligible voter submits yes, no, or abstain in the designated record before
the deadline. A vote may be changed before closing, with the final choice
counted. The decision owner verifies eligibility, conflicts, quorum, threshold,
and totals, then records the outcome, date, rationale, and any dissent. A public
record should not expose confidential or security-sensitive information.

The maintainers own this process and review it alongside the voting policies.
