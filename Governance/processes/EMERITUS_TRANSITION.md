# Emeritus Transition Process

## Purpose

This process describes a voluntary transition from an active governance role to
emeritus status. The eligibility, recognition, and retained privileges of the
emeritus role are defined in [EMERITUS.md](../roles/EMERITUS.md).

## Transition

1. The role-holder contacts a maintainer or opens a governance issue requesting
   emeritus status, effective date, and any handover needs. They may request a
   private discussion.
2. The role owner and role-holder identify active decisions, work, and access
   requiring transfer. Handover follows the
   [Offboarding Process](OFFBOARDING.md).
3. Maintainers confirm eligibility and retained access under the Emeritus role
   definition. Any conflict is disclosed and the conflicted maintainer recuses.
4. The decision and effective date are recorded in the appropriate public role
   record. Private personal or security details are not included.
5. Access not explicitly retained by the Emeritus role is removed or reduced by
   the effective date. No former authority is retained by implication.

## Return to Active Status

An emeritus member may request an active role by contacting the role owner. The
request is evaluated against the current appointment or promotion criteria for
that specific role; prior service is relevant evidence but does not restore
privileges automatically. Approval and access provisioning are recorded before
active duties resume.

## Ownership

The maintainers coordinate transitions and own this process. Changes follow the
governance change process.
