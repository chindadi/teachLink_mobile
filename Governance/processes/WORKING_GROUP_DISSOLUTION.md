# Working Group Dissolution Process

## Triggers

A working group may be dissolved when its chartered deliverables are complete,
its purpose is no longer needed, the approved term ends without an extension,
the group cannot maintain a lead or sufficient participation, or the maintainers
determine that its scope should move elsewhere. Dissolution is not a sanction;
conduct or access concerns follow their applicable policies.

## Decision and Notice

The working group lead proposes closure in the group's tracking issue with the
trigger, status of each deliverable, open risks, and recommended owners for
remaining work. Any group member may request dissolution if the lead is
unavailable. The maintainers decide when closure is disputed or the charter
assigns approval to them. Give members at least 7 calendar days to raise a
handover concern unless urgent security or continuity needs require faster
action.

## Handover and Archive

Before closure, the lead and members:

- complete or explicitly defer deliverables and record acceptance or remaining
  work;
- assign owners for ongoing services, issues, decisions, and dependencies;
- transfer project-owned documentation and artifacts to their long-term owner;
- return or revoke project credentials and access that are no longer required;
- publish a final summary with outcomes, open follow-ups, and lessons learned.

The maintainers archive the charter, status reports, and final summary in the
repository's designated governance or project archive. Sensitive or personal
records remain restricted. The group is considered dissolved when handover is
recorded, necessary access is reconciled, and the archive location is linked
from the formation record.

The maintainers own this process and review it with the working group formation
process.
