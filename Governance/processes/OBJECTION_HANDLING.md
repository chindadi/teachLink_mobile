# Objection Handling Process

## Purpose

This process defines how objections are raised and resolved for TeachLink Mobile.
It gives contributors and maintainers a clear, fair mechanism to challenge
decisions without blocking progress indefinitely.

## Scope

This process applies to decisions made within the TeachLink Mobile repository and
its governance, including technical direction, release, and policy decisions.

## How Objections Are Raised

- Anyone may raise an objection to a decision by opening an issue or adding a
  comment that references the decision being challenged.
- The objection should state what is being objected to and why, including any
  technical, safety, privacy, or community harm the objector expects.
- An objection should be raised promptly. Late objections raised only at the point
  of merge are subject to expedited handling as described below.

## Resolution Steps

- The decision owner or reviewer evaluates the objection and confirms they
  understand the concern.
- The parties attempt to resolve the objection through discussion on the issue or
  thread, considering data, tests, and project priorities.
- If agreement is reached, the outcome is documented on the thread and the
  decision proceeds or is adjusted accordingly.
- If the objection is not resolvable in discussion, it is escalated per the
  escalation path below.

## Escalation Path

- Unresolved objections are escalated to a maintainer who was not involved in the
  original decision.
- The escalating maintainer reviews the objection, weighs the evidence, and makes
  a final decision in a timely manner.
- The decision and its rationale are documented publicly so the community can see
  how the objection was handled.
- The outcome does not prevent a new objection with new evidence from being raised.

## Ownership

- Maintainers own this process and are responsible for timely resolution.
- Changes to this process are proposed in a pull request that touches only the
  `Governance/` folder.

## Success

This process succeeds when objections are heard, resolved fairly, and documented,
and when the project can keep moving without overriding legitimate concerns.
