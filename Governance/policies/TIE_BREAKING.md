# Tie-Breaking Policy

## Scope

This policy applies when an eligible, quorate formal vote on a project decision
has equal yes and no votes. It does not change a decision rule that expressly
requires a supermajority.

## Casting Vote

For a simple-majority vote, the non-conflicted chair named in the vote notice
holds the casting vote. The chair is ordinarily the community lead; if that
person is unavailable or conflicted, the eligible maintainers name an
uninvolved maintainer as chair before the vote closes. The chair records the
casting vote and a brief rationale with the result.

The casting vote is not used to satisfy a supermajority threshold. If no
eligible chair can be named, or a tie remains under a required threshold, the
motion is not approved and is deferred for further discussion or revision.
Silence, abstentions, and failure to reach quorum are not ties.

The vote record includes the original totals, the chair's identity, and whether a
casting vote was used. The maintainers own this policy.
