# Supermajority Policy

## Decisions Requiring a Supermajority

The following maintainer decisions require a supermajority:

- amending or replacing the project charter;
- appointing or removing a maintainer;
- permanently revoking a maintainer's governance privileges;
- dissolving the project or transferring its stewardship.

Code of Conduct sanctions remain governed by the
[`Code of Conduct Enforcement Policy`](../COC_ENFORCEMENT.md), including its
independent review safeguards; this policy does not alter that process.

## Threshold

At least two-thirds of valid yes/no votes must be yes. The count is rounded up:
`yes >= ceil(2 * (yes + no) / 3)`. Abstentions count toward quorum but are not
included among valid yes/no votes. The vote must also meet the
[`quorum policy`](QUORUM.md). A casting vote cannot lower this threshold.

The vote notice must identify that a supermajority applies and link to the
proposal and supporting evidence. If the threshold or quorum is not met, the
motion is not approved and may be revised and resubmitted. The decision record
states the threshold, totals, and outcome. The maintainers own this policy.
