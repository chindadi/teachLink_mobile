# Inactivity Policy

## Purpose

This policy sets review thresholds for project roles whose duties require
ongoing participation. Inactivity triggers a check-in, not automatic removal.
Approved leave, an announced pause, and emeritus status are not inactivity.

## Thresholds

| Role or access               | Inactivity threshold                                                                        |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Maintainer                   | 90 consecutive days without a governance response, review, or documented leave              |
| Committer                    | 90 consecutive days without a merge or documented leave                                     |
| Reviewer                     | 90 consecutive days without a review or documented leave                                    |
| Triager                      | 90 consecutive days without a triage action or documented leave                             |
| Other appointed roles        | 90 consecutive days without a role duty, unless the role document sets a different interval |
| Repository or service access | 90 consecutive days unused, subject to operational and security requirements                |

The role owner may consider the role's actual workload and seasonal cadence. A
single missed task or delayed response is not, by itself, grounds for removal.

## Notification and Review

The role owner or a maintainer sends a private check-in when the threshold is
reached, identifies the duties or access under review, and allows at least 14
days for a response. The person may confirm they remain active, request leave,
or ask to step down. If there is no response, send one final notice and allow
another 7 days before recommending a role or access change.

## Outcomes and Reinstatement

Possible outcomes are no change, an agreed leave or reduced scope, transfer of
duties, or a revocation review under
[`REVOCATION.md`](REVOCATION.md). Access needed for an active security or
operational responsibility may be reviewed separately and promptly. A former
role-holder may request reinstatement by contacting the role owner; appointment
or promotion criteria for that role still apply.

The maintainers own this policy and review it annually. Changes follow the
governance change process.
