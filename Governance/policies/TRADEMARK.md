# Trademark and Brand Usage Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how the TeachLink trademarks and brand assets may be used by
the community, partners, and the project itself, and how permission is granted for
uses not allowed by default.

## Scope

This policy applies to the TeachLink name, logo, wordmark, app icons, and any other
marks or brand assets published by the project. It does not apply to the trademarks
of third parties that may be referenced by the app.

## Protected Marks

The project's protected marks include the TeachLink name, the wordmark, the app
icon and logo, and distinctive taglines or campaign marks introduced in official
documentation. These marks identify the project and its product and may not be
registered or claimed by others in connection with software or related services.

## Permitted Uses

The following uses are permitted without further approval:

- identifying TeachLink as a dependency or integration in a product, website, or
  course in a nominative way;
- linking to the project repository, documentation, or store listings;
- using the name in factual, non-misleading commentary, reviews, or news coverage;
  and
- reproducing official brand assets from the published asset location for the uses
  permitted in this section.

## Brand Usage Guidelines

### Logo and Name Usage

The logo and wordmark are used only from the official asset set: the primary logo,
the monochrome variant, and the app icon. The mark is not stretched, recolored,
rotated, outlined, or separated into parts. The name is written exactly as
TeachLink, with the prescribed casing, and is not combined with other words into a
new mark or an implied association.

### Do and Don't

Do:

- keep clear space around the mark as specified in the published brand sheets; and
- use the full-color logo on the provided light or dark backgrounds.

Don't:

- imply endorsement or partnership without written permission;
- use the mark in a way that suggests modified builds, patches, or rebuilds are
  official; or
- animate, warp, or layer effects over the logo, or place it on busy backgrounds
  that reduce legibility.

### Asset Location

Official brand assets are published in the repository's `assets/` folder and on the
project website, and are versioned alongside the release artifacts.

## Request Process for Other Uses

Uses not covered by the permitted list require written approval. A request is made
by opening an issue in the repository detailing the use, the audience, and the
timeframe. The maintainers review requests in the regular release cadence and
respond in writing; approval may include conditions and is revocable if the use
becomes misleading. Approved uses are recorded for transparency.

## Ownership

The maintainers own this policy. Changes are proposed in a pull request that
touches only the `Governance/` folder.

## Success

This policy succeeds when community and partner use of the brand is consistent,
fair, and clearly understood, and when misuse is rare and quickly clarified.
