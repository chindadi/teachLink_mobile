# Voting Quorum Policy

## Scope

This policy defines quorum for formal project-wide maintainer votes. A working
group may use a different quorum only when its approved charter states the rule;
that rule cannot replace a maintainer decision required by the charter.

## Eligible Voters and Threshold

Eligible voters are active maintainers who are not conflicted on the question.
The voter list is fixed when the vote opens. Quorum is reached when at least half
of those eligible voters, rounded up, submit a vote of yes, no, or abstain before
the deadline. An abstention counts toward quorum but not toward the yes/no
decision threshold. A recused person is excluded from both the eligible count and
quorum calculation.

Unless a policy requires a supermajority, a motion passes when yes votes exceed
no votes. A tie does not pass and is handled under the
[`tie-breaking policy`](TIE_BREAKING.md). The vote remains subject to the
[`supermajority policy`](SUPERMAJORITY.md) where applicable.

## When Quorum Is Not Met

No decision is recorded. The decision owner may extend the vote once for up to 7
calendar days, seek additional discussion, or defer the question. After an
extension without quorum, the motion is deferred and may be reopened with a new
notice period; silence is never counted as approval.

The record identifies the question, eligible voter count, response count, yes/no
totals, abstentions, deadline, and outcome. The maintainers own this policy.
