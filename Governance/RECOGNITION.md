# Contributor Recognition and Credits Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how TeachLink Mobile recognizes contributions and credits
contributors. It makes recognition fair, transparent, and consistent so that
everyone who helps the project is acknowledged.

## Scope

This policy applies to all contributors to the TeachLink Mobile repository and to
all contributions covered by the project's contribution guidelines.

## Recognition Tiers

Contributors are recognized in three tiers:

- **Contributor** — first accepted contribution that is more than iterative: a
  merged pull request, a substantively reviewed and adopted proposal, or a
  documented security report.
- **Active Maintainer** — sustained, trusted participation in reviews, maintenance,
  and triage over a sustained period, recognized by maintainer nomination.
- **Core Leader** — long-standing leadership with responsibility for an area of the
  project, recognized by consensus of the maintainers.

## Criteria per Tier

- **Contributor**: the contribution is merged and the contributor is traceable
  under the project's sign-off policy.
- **Active Maintainer**: a history of regular, constructive review and maintenance;
  adherence to the code of conduct; and demonstrated reliability over several
  release cycles.
- **Core Leader**: significant technical or community leadership, sustained
  involvement, and peer confidence; subject to the conflict-of-interest and conduct
  policies like any contributor.

## Nomination Process

Anyone may nominate a contributor. A nomination states the person, the proposed
tier, and the evidence (merged work, reviews, reports). A tier change requires a
second from a maintainer and acceptance by a majority of the maintainers, and the
decision is announced in the project's contribution channel. Self-nomination is not
used for the maintainer or core-leader tiers.

## Credits and Acknowledgements

### How Contributions Are Credited

Every accepted contribution is credited to its contributors. Recognition includes a
mention in the release notes for the release containing the work, a link to the
contributor's profile for authored work, and a record in the credits file. A
contributor may request removal from public credit at any time.

### Credits File Format

Credits are recorded in the `CREDITS.md` file in the repository root. Each entry
lists the contributor's name or handle, a link to their profile, and the area or
theme of their contributions. Entries are ordered alphabetically and written in
plain markdown so the file is easy to review.

### Update Cadence

The credits file is updated at least at every release, and each accepted
contribution deserving credit is added within one release cycle of being merged.
Removals and corrections are processed on request and reviewed for the following
release.

## Ownership

The maintainers own this policy. The community lead administers nominations and
keeps the credits file current. Changes are proposed in a pull request that touches
only the `Governance/` folder.

## Success

This policy succeeds when participation is consistently and fairly acknowledged,
nominations are handled transparently, and the credits file reflects the people who
built the project.
