# Maintainer Role

## Purpose

Maintainers steward TeachLink Mobile's direction, technical quality, and
governance. This document defines their decision authority and accountability;
it complements the [Project Charter](../CHARTER.md).

## Responsibilities

Maintainers:

- set and communicate project priorities in line with the charter and scope;
- review or delegate review of significant code, release, security, and
  governance decisions;
- ensure required checks, approvals, and documentation are complete before
  authorizing a merge or release;
- support contributors and resolve escalated disputes using the documented
  processes;
- maintain role assignments, repository permissions, and succession plans;
- document material decisions, rationale, conflicts, and dissent in the
  appropriate issue, pull request, or restricted record;
- protect confidential reports and recuse from matters where they have a
  personal or material conflict.

## Decision Authority

Maintainers are the final repository governance authority where no more specific
policy assigns the decision elsewhere. They may approve governance changes,
priorities, releases, role appointments, and operational actions within the
charter and applicable policies. They must not bypass required security, privacy,
review, or CI gates. Routine decisions may use lazy consensus where permitted;
formal votes, quorum, ties, and supermajorities follow the corresponding
governance policies.

Maintainers do not act alone on a matter where a specific policy requires
independent review, a panel, or a higher voting threshold. Emergency restrictions
must be limited to what is necessary and reviewed under the
[Revocation Policy](../policies/REVOCATION.md) or applicable conduct process.

## Accountability

Maintainers are expected to:

- explain decisions in a timely, respectful, and evidence-based manner;
- apply policies consistently and avoid retaliation or preferential treatment;
- disclose conflicts and recuse when impartiality is compromised;
- protect private and security-sensitive information;
- seek community input on material proposals and record outcomes;
- review their own capacity and request leave or transition when they cannot
  meet role duties.

## Appointment, Review, and Removal

Appointments follow the [Role Nomination Process](../processes/NOMINATION.md)
and the criteria in
[`PROMOTION_CRITERIA.md`](../processes/PROMOTION_CRITERIA.md). Maintainers
review role coverage annually. A maintainer may resign at any time. Removal or
permanent revocation follows the nomination/decision requirements and
[`REVOCATION.md`](../policies/REVOCATION.md), including conflict-free review,
notice, and appeal. A temporary access restriction may be imposed only as
allowed by the applicable security or conduct process.

The maintainers collectively own this role definition and review it annually.
